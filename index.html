ABOUT THE GAME

Snail Out is built around one simple goal: every snail must reach the correct shell.

You control snails of different colors and sizes, guiding them through narrow paths, intersections, and obstacles.

Each level is a small logic maze where timing and order matter just as much as direction.

It’s easy to understand — and surprisingly tricky to master.

HOW THE SNAILS MOVE

Every level asks you to think before you slide.

  • The controls are intentionally simple:

    • Drag from a snail’s body

    • Slide it across the board

    • Release and let it glide

  • But the challenge comes from everything around it.

    • Snails can block each other

    • Paths overlap and cross

    • One wrong move can trap another snail

PUZZLES THAT GROW WITH YOU

Snail Out introduces difficulty gradually:

  • Early levels teach movement and matching

  • Mid-level puzzles introduce tighter paths and timing pressure

  • Advanced stages require multi-snail planning and perfect order

When you fail, it's because you messed up, not because the game cheated you - which is honestly refreshing.

TIMING, PRESSURE & SATISFACTION

Some levels let you relax and plan slowly. Others push you to move quickly and react. This mix creates:

  • Tension when paths are crowded

  • That “almost failed” feeling that makes success sweeter

  • The game balances calm thinking with exciting near-misses.

  • Relief when the last snail escapes

LEVEL VARIETY & REPLAY VALUE

  • Dozens of handcrafted puzzles

  • Different board layouts and path designs

  • Increasing complexity without repeating ideas

  • New surprises unlocked as you progress

  • Each level feels like a fresh problem to solve.

WHO THIS GAME IS FOR

Snail Out is perfect for players who:

  • Like games that are cute but clever

  • Want short, satisfying puzzle sessions

  • It’s friendly on the surface — smart underneath.

  • Enjoy logic and escape-style puzzles

  • Prefer thinking and planning over fast tapping

  • Appreciate clean design and smooth controls
